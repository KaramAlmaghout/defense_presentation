# Speaker notes for main2.pdf

Target speaking time: **about 18 minutes**, leaving roughly two minutes for
transitions and minor delays. The main defense contains 17 slides; the detailed
model, parameter studies, complete result tables, and comparative studies begin
after the closing slide.

Do not derive the equations or read tables row by row. For Slides 7 and 8,
explain the physical meaning of the equations while pointing to the figures.

| Slide | Time | Speaking focus |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 0:20 | State the exact dissertation title and specialty. |
| 2 | 1:00 | Define a deformable linear object (DLO), then explain the three difficulties: complete-shape control, uncertain mechanics, and whole-cable collision safety. |
| 3 | 1:00 | State the goal and summarize the five research objectives. |
| 4 | 1:00 | Formulate the two problems. Distinguish planned reference profiles from the executed shape, and static cable validity from transition validity. |
| 5 | 1:00 | State the five scientific contributions in the same order as the dissertation chapters. |
| 6 | 1:00 | Follow the seven VFP steps. Emphasize that the cable is marker-free; only one tool is marked to establish point order. |
| 7 | 1:35 | Explain ALI: choose the maximally displaced point, interpolate that point and all segment angles, then reconstruct toward both ends. Point to the linear-interpolation failure and ALI result. |
| 8 | 1:30 | Explain the diminishing-rigidity idea: a taut point follows the gripper strongly; slack reduces coupling. Then present the dissertation's reported stage formulation and state that constrained optimization bounds robot velocities; do not claim monotonic convergence. |
| 9 | 1:10 | Report both simulation and physical evidence. Say explicitly: all 32 simulation tasks completed, six hit the cap before strict convergence, and all tested physical transformations completed. |
| 10 | 1:00 | Define an ordered cable configuration and the required start-to-goal route. Explain why every neighboring transition must be checked. |
| 11 | 1:15 | Walk through the GCR sequence: two trees, guided complete-shape sample, APF bias, projected relaxation, validity checks, and shortcut refinement. |
| 12 | 1:00 | Explain the swept quadrilateral for each corresponding cable segment and the endpoint-order check. |
| 13 | 1:00 | Report the protocol and headline results: 10 scenarios × 1000 seeds, 10,000 valid tested routes, timing range, and path refinement. Qualify this as empirical evidence. |
| 14 | 0:55 | State the two-UR10 MuJoCo setup and the final-shape RMSE and maximum-error ranges. Make clear that GCR validation is simulation-only. |
| 15 | 1:05 | Summarize the scientific results and state the established limitations without adding new claims. |
| 16 | 0:50 | State five publications, the three conferences, and Russian Science Foundation support. |
| 17 | 0:10 | Thank the committee and invite questions. |

Suggested transition into the appendix: “The detailed DLO model, simulation
parameter studies, complete experimental tables, specialty traceability, and
comparative studies are available in the backup slides.”
